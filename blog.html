Maintenance Tips

Quick guides to keep your property in top condition.

Practical advice for homeowners, landlords, and property managers to prevent common repair issues.

Keep drains clear

Use strainers, avoid grease down the sink, and schedule regular checks to prevent costly blockages.

Test safety devices

Check smoke alarms, circuit breakers and emergency lighting to keep your property safe and compliant.

Protect your paint

Repair small cracks and refresh worn areas to stop moisture damage before it spreads.

Inspect roofs regularly

Look for loose tiles, gutter clogs and signs of leaks after storms to avoid expensive repairs.
